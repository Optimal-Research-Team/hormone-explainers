The hormone cycle and its stop points

Hormones feed each other in a loop, so a problem in one spreads to the rest. Each stop on the ring is a place where the loop can be interrupted, with the tools most often used there.

1
Sleep

Daridorexant, lemborexant (Dayvigo), doxepin, glycine, ashwagandha, progesterone, lifestyle.

2
Insulin resistance

Metformin, GLP-1 agonists.

3
Cortisol

DHEA, omega-3, rhodiola, magnesium and B6, ashwagandha, circadian rhythm, exercise, lifestyle.

4
Melatonin

Light exposure, tryptophan, B6, folate, magnesium, exercise, temperature.

5
Estrogen

Progesterone.

6
Mood, fatigue, cognition

SSRI (escitalopram), benzodiazepine (alprazolam), creatine 5 g/day.
