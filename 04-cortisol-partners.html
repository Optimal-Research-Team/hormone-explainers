Cortisol's partners

Cortisol does not act alone. Some molecules turn down its production, some soften its effects once released, and some sit on the other end of the see-saw.

UPSTREAM · ACT ON CRH, ACTH OR THE ADRENAL

CortisolIts own negative feedback loop.
Oxytocin↓ ACTH and ↓ cortisol, at rest and under stress; dose dependent. Also calms the stress response in the brain.
ANPAtrial natriuretic peptide: ↓ CRH, ↓ ACTH, ↓ cortisol.
Beta-endorphin↓ ACTH, ↓ cortisol.
GABAQuiets the hypothalamic drive.
EndocannabinoidsDampen HPA activation.
MelatoninOpposes cortisol across the day–night cycle.
DOWNSTREAM
Antagonise cortisol's effect

Cortisol is still released, but its downstream actions are buffered.

THE SEE-SAW
Melatonin vs. cortisol

When one is high the other should be low. Evening cortisol that stays up keeps melatonin down, and sleep suffers.
