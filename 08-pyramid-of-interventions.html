The pyramid of interventions

Start at the base. Once routines and stability are built, effort can move up. Marketing will tell you the top layer cures all; it only works on top of the very basics.

4
Exercise type and timing; calories and macros

Once routines are stable, fine-tuning exercise and shifting effort to a diet or meal plan brings further benefit.

3
Eating regularly; nutrient deficiencies; insulin resistance

Erratic eating and prolonged fasting can worsen HPA dysfunction. Correct deficiencies and treat insulin resistance, including GLP-1 or metformin where appropriate.

2
Exercise, basics, mindfulness, social connection

Exercise you enjoy and will do consistently beats any specific type. Basics: hydration, fibre, magnesium, B12, vitamin D, iron, folate. Mindfulness and connection lift oxytocin, which repairs the HPA axis.

1
Sleep and circadian rhythm recovery Start here

Dark, cool (16–19 °C) and quiet (under 33 dB). REM sleep ties to cortisol, insulin resistance, mood, energy and cognition. Rebuilding the cortisol curve needs morning sunlight and dim evenings.
