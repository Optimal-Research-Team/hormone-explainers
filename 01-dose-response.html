Cortisol has a dose-dependent effect

Too little and too much cortisol both cause harm. The body needs a middle band, where cortisol does its everyday work of regulating energy, inflammation, blood pressure and mood.

Too little

Deficiency
  • Vasodilation, ↓ blood pressure
  • ↓ glucose, ↓ free fatty acids, ↓ amino acids
  • ↑ cytokines, fever; altered immune cells
  • Malaise, fatigue
  • ↓ Na⁺, ↑ K⁺
  • Anorexia, weight loss, nausea, vomiting
On the curve

Just right

Normal actions
  • Gluconeogenesis and glycogenesis
  • Protein and fat catabolism; peripheral glucose uptake
  • Vascular tone; Na⁺ / K⁺ balance
  • Suppresses inflammation (IL-1, IL-6, TNF)
  • Modulates immune cells: ↓ leukocytes and eosinophils, ↑ neutrophils
  • Cognition, memory, mood, circadian rhythm
On the curve

Too much

Excess
  • Muscle catabolism, proximal myopathy
  • Osteoporosis, fragility fractures, poor Ca absorption
  • Skin fragility, striae, bruising, slow wound healing
  • Memory impairment, anxiety, depression
  • ↓ GnRH, ↓ growth hormone, central hypothyroidism
  • ↑ glucose, insulin resistance, visceral fat, atherosclerosis
