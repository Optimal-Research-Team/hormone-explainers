Chapter 05 / 08 · Priorities

Foundational vs. top-line hormones

Think of your hormones as a tree. Sex hormones are the canopy everyone notices, but they grow from roots and a trunk. If the roots are unwell, treating the leaves will not fix the tree.

Fig. 05 · The hormone tree

Healthy roots: the whole tree has what it needs.

Canopy · top line First to suffer
Testosterone, DHEA, estrogen, progesterone

The hormones most people ask about. They are the visible outcome of everything below: the first to suffer when the foundation is off, and often the last to recover.

Trunk
Insulin

Carries energy from the roots to the canopy. Insulin resistance thickens the trunk the wrong way, driving fat storage, inflammation and imbalances in the sex hormones above.

Roots · foundational Unwell
Cortisol and thyroid

Set the pace of metabolism and the stress response for every cell. Stabilise these first: rhythm, sleep, nutrition and stress load. Everything above draws from them.
